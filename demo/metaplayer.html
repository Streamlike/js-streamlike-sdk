Streamlike JS SDK - Meta Player Demo

This page demonstrates generateMetaPlayer: the Streamlike player without its own interface, driven from the page, with a jog wheel, 21 playback speeds, zoom inside the picture, layers (the comments on the timeline, the tags in the picture) and the audience curve over the progress bar.

Drag or scroll the wheel to browse by the second. Scroll over the picture to zoom, drag to move, double click to come back. Hover the progress bar to open the audience curve. The comments on the right and their markers answer to each other.
